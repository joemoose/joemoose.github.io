Advantage & Disadvantage via a ±1 Modifier

PbtA outcome odds when advantage is a flat +1 and disadvantage a flat −1 on the total, always rolling 2d6.

Success — 10+ Partial — 7–9 Failure — 6−
Disadvantage2d6 + attribute − 1
Normal2d6 + attribute
Advantage2d6 + attribute + 1

How to read this. Each line is set apart three ways at once — colour, dash pattern, and marker shape — so the three outcomes stay distinct when printed in black and white. Bands: a total of 10 or more is a success, 7 to 9 a partial, 6 or less a failure. Hover any point for exact percentages.
