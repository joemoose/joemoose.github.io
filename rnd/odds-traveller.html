2d6 + Modifier ≥ 8 — Success Probability

Roll 2d6 and add the modifier. 8 or higher succeeds; 7 or lower fails. Modifier runs −3 to +5 along the bottom. Hover or tap any point for exact odds.

Success Failure
Threshold is fixed at 8+; Success and Failure always sum to 100%.
Caveat: raising the target to 9+ doesn't require a separate dial — it's mathematically identical to keeping the 8+ target and shifting every modifier down by 1 (e.g. 9+ at +2 gives the same odds as 8+ at +1). The two only diverge at the very bottom of the range, where a 9+ target reaches one modifier step further down (to −4) than this chart's −3 floor covers.
