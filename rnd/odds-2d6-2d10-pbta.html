PbtA Modifier Scaling: 2d6 vs. 2d10

How the odds of failure, partial success, and full success change as the modifier grows, and where each system “breaks.”

Hover for exact values. Click the chart, a table row, or use the slider to select a modifier.

Each probability is computed by enumerating every possible roll (36 for 2d6, 100 for 2d10) with the modifier added to the total. Average damage uses the higher die on a success and the lower die on a partial; a failure deals none.
