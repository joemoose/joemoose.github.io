Nearly all characters are playable through level 13. By level 22, about 51% have transmogrified and 49% have been obliterated.

Percentages are cumulative and exact (calculated, not simulated). They assume the character keeps making Corruption Saves, one per level.

The rules behind the curves

Roll d20 against your corruption level. On a result lower than your level, roll d20 + your level on the Manifestations table. Either way, your level then rises by 1.

A result of 18–33 is a Vitiation. Your sixth Vitiation of any type transmogrifies you. A result of 34+ opens the portal and obliterates you.
