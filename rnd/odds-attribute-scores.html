Ability Score Curves

Exact probabilities for seven ways to generate a 3–18 attribute score, computed by enumerating every dice outcome. Click a method to hide it, hover or use the arrow keys to compare scores.

How each method is computed

  • 3d6 Sum of three six-sided dice.
  • 4d6, drop lowest Roll four d6, discard the lowest die, sum the other three.
  • Sezrekan Method Roll 4d7, count any 7 as a 6, discard the lowest die, sum the rest.
  • Tatterdemalion's Tampering Roll a d5 and 3d7. On 1–2 count each 7 as a 1; on 3 reroll 7s (identical to 3d6); on 4–5 count each 7 as a 6. The curve is the weighted mix 2/5, 1/5, 2/5.
  • 2d10, clamped Sum of two d10, with results below 3 set to 3 and results above 18 set to 18.
  • d16+2 One d16 plus 2, so every score from 3 to 18 is equally likely.
  • 2d6+6 Sum of two d6 plus 6, giving scores from 8 to 18 only.
