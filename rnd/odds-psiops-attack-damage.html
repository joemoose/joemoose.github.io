PSIOPS Attack & Damage Probabilities

One roll sets both quality and severity: success deals the higher die, a partial deals the lower die, a failure deals nothing.

Hover or tap a bar for the exact figure. Click a legend item to show or hide a system.

Every possible roll is enumerated (36 for 2d6, 100 for 2d10), so these are exact probabilities, not simulations. The modifier is added to the dice total before the bands are applied.
