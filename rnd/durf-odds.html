d20 + Modifier ± d6 Pool — Success Probability

Mechanic: d20 + modifier + pool ≥ 16 succeeds. The pool is the single highest die among n d6 (n ranges from −1, subtract a d6, to +3, add three d6 and keep the best). Modifier runs +1 to +8 along the bottom. Hover or tap any point for the exact odds.

−1d6 No d6 (d20 only) +1d6 +2d6 (highest) +3d6 (highest)
Success threshold is fixed at 16+. For pools of 2 or more d6, only the highest single die counts — the rest are discarded.
